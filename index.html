Privacy Policy

Location Information

The app may request access to your device's location to display your current position on the map. Location information is processed locally on your device and is not sent to our servers or stored by us.

Location Permissions

Location access is optional. You can allow or deny location permission through your device settings. Some location-based features may not work if permission is denied.

The app does not send your device's location to our servers. This statement does not cover information that may be independently processed by third-party services used by the app, such as map providers.
